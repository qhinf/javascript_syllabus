# Project Overview: JavaScript Course for the AI Era

## Objective
Develop a comprehensive JavaScript course tailored for students aged 15–18 who are already studying computer science and programming.

## Core Philosophy & Pedagogical Approach
In response to the rise of Artificial Intelligence, the course shifts focus from traditional "code-from-scratch" learning to a modern approach that prepares students to work alongside AI tools. The emphasis is on:
* **Code Comprehension:** Learning to read and deeply understand JavaScript.
* **Logic Completion:** Developing the ability to fill in missing pieces of a program (gap-filling).
* **Debugging:** Mastering the art of identifying, tracing, and fixing errors in existing code.
* **Critical Analysis:** Empowering students to critically evaluate and oversee code generated by AI.

## Target Audience
* **Age:** 15–18 years old.
* **Background:** Students with an existing interest or foundation in computer science and programming.

## Current Progress
* **Chapter 1 Completed:** "Introductie tot JavaScript" (Introduction to JavaScript). This chapter covers the importance of JavaScript, its ubiquity (social media, streaming, games, etc.), and introduces the new pedagogical focus on AI-era programming.
